Coverage notes

East and North-East listings were added in this update, and none is within 245m of an open station. Regions follow URA, except South, which here means Bukit Merah (Redhill and Tiong Bahru), a part of URA's Central region. Punggol and Sengkang are left out because their closest listings were measured to LRT stations, not MRT.

The 8m listing at Redhill MRT is the closest in the set and is worth checking on a map. 135 Jalan Bukit Merah appeared at several prices for the same size, so only the lowest is kept. Floor area in sqm is converted from the platform's sqft.

Stations marked not open yet are hidden by default, because a listing near a future station is not near a running one. They are Choa Chu Kang West on the Jurong Region Line (targeted for mid-2028) and Pasir Ris East, Tampines North and Serangoon North on the planned Cross Island Line. The Cross Island Line stations are an assumption from their CR codes, so check LTA for dates. Untick Open stations only to see them.

The town median column compares the asking price with HDB's 3Q 2026 median for the town and flat type, as reported by Dollars and Sense and Little Big Red Dot. It is only available for six listings and is a rough guide, since medians mix flats of every size and age.

Lease left is estimated as 99 minus the years since the built year. The monthly figure is a simple repayment estimate from the loan assumptions above, not a loan offer or advice. The median comparison uses captured listings of the same region and type and only appears when there are three or more.

Left out for data quality: 53 Lorong 5 Toa Payoh (labelled 5-room but listed at 731 sqft) and 75 Marine Drive (no distance shown). Duplicate listings seen on several result pages were merged by address and unit size.

The Marine Parade unit without an address has a price worked out from its psf and size.
